# Vocab Lookup — How to Use

This skill helps you look up English words, phrases, and idioms **in the exact context of a source text you provide**.

---

## Step 1 — Provide your source

Paste any source text you are reading or studying. It can be:

- A passage or article
- A video transcript
- A document or report
- A book excerpt
- Any other English text

**Example:**

```
[Paste your source text here]

The rapid expansion of cloud computing has fundamentally altered how organizations approach
data storage and software deployment. Rather than maintaining costly on-premise infrastructure,
companies now leverage scalable, pay-as-you-go services offered by major providers.
```

The assistant will confirm receipt and wait for your word queries.

---

## Step 2 — Ask about any word or phrase

Simply type the word, phrase, or idiom you want to look up. You can ask about one or many at once.

**Single word:**
> leverage

**Multiple words at once:**
> leverage, scalable, pay-as-you-go

**A phrase or idiom:**
> pay-as-you-go

---

## Sample Output

For the source above, asking about `leverage` would return:

```
leverage
――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――
Meaning:        tận dụng, khai thác (lợi thế / công cụ sẵn có)
Pronunciation:  /ˈlevərɪdʒ/
Part of speech: verb
Example:        "Companies now leverage scalable, pay-as-you-go services offered by major providers."
                (source: quoted from source)
Synonyms:       utilize; harness; exploit; capitalize on; make use of
```

---

## Notes

- You do **not** need to paste the source again for each word query. The assistant remembers it throughout the session.
- If a word has more than one relevant meaning in your source, you will receive **one card per meaning**, each with a different example and different synonyms.
- If you ask about a word that does not appear in the source, the assistant will note this and still provide a card using a similar context.
