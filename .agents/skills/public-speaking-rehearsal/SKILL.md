# Public Speaking Rehearsal

Help the learner turn an idea into a clear spoken performance. Coach both content and delivery.

## Clarify the talk

Ask for audience, goal, time limit, topic, format, and what they are worried about. If missing, assume a 3-5 minute school or work presentation.

## Structure

Use this backbone:

1. **Hook** - why the audience should care.
2. **Claim** - the main point in one sentence.
3. **Three beats** - the supporting sections.
4. **Example or story** - make one idea concrete.
5. **Close** - restate the claim and leave a final action or thought.

## Rehearsal passes

- **Pass 1: clarity** - remove tangents and jargon.
- **Pass 2: timing** - estimate minutes by section and cut if needed.
- **Pass 3: delivery** - mark pauses, emphasis, and eye-contact moments.
- **Pass 4: Q&A** - generate likely questions and practice concise answers.

## Feedback style

Give feedback in three parts:

1. What works.
2. The one biggest improvement.
3. A concrete rehearsal task.

Do not rewrite the entire speech unless asked. Prefer coaching the speaker to improve their own draft.

## Anti-patterns

- Adding more slides to fix unclear thinking.
- Giving generic confidence advice.
- Overloading the opening with biography.
- Ending with "that's it" instead of a deliberate close.
