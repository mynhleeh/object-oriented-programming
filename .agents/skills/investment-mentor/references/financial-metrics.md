# Financial Metrics Glossary

For each metric, teach: what it means, why investors care, good vs bad,
historical trend, and what could manipulate it.

## Revenue
- **What it means:** Total money from selling products/services before costs.
- **Why investors care:** Top-line growth signals demand and market share.
- **Good vs bad:** Consistent growth beats flat/declining; quality of growth
  (organic vs one-off) matters.
- **Trend:** Compare YoY and multi-year CAGR.
- **Manipulation:** Channel stuffing, aggressive revenue recognition, one-time
  items, acquisitions masking organic decline.

## Gross Margin
- **What it means:** (Revenue − COGS) / Revenue. Profit after direct costs.
- **Why investors care:** Shows pricing power and production efficiency.
- **Good vs bad:** High and stable is strong; falling margins signal
  competition or cost pressure.
- **Trend:** Watch direction over several quarters.
- **Manipulation:** Capitalizing costs, shifting COGS to opex, inventory
  accounting changes.

## Operating Margin
- **What it means:** Operating income / Revenue. Profit after operating costs.
- **Why investors care:** Reflects core business efficiency and management
  discipline.
- **Good vs bad:** High and rising is ideal; low or volatile is a concern.
- **Trend:** Compare to peers and history.
- **Manipulation:** One-time gains, R&D cuts, aggressive depreciation.

## Net Income
- **What it means:** Bottom-line profit after all expenses and taxes.
- **Why investors care:** The headline profitability number.
- **Good vs bad:** Positive and growing; watch quality (recurring vs one-off).
- **Trend:** Multi-year trajectory.
- **Manipulation:** Tax adjustments, one-time items, non-cash charges.

## Free Cash Flow (FCF)
- **What it means:** Operating cash flow − capital expenditures.
- **Why investors care:** Real cash available to shareholders; harder to fake
  than earnings.
- **Good vs bad:** Strong positive FCF funds dividends, buybacks, growth.
- **Trend:** FCF conversion vs net income.
- **Manipulation:** Delaying capex, stretching payables, factoring receivables.

## Debt
- **What it means:** Total borrowings (short + long term).
- **Why investors care:** Leverage affects solvency and flexibility.
- **Good vs bad:** Manageable vs net debt; compare to EBITDA and cash.
- **Trend:** Rising debt with falling cash flow is a red flag.
- **Manipulation:** Off-balance-sheet debt, leases, pension obligations.

## ROIC (Return on Invested Capital)
- **What it means:** NOPAT / Invested Capital. Return on all capital deployed.
- **Why investors care:** Best measure of value creation and moat.
- **Good vs bad:** ROIC above cost of capital creates value; below destroys it.
- **Trend:** Sustained high ROIC signals a durable advantage.
- **Manipulation:** Capitalizing expenses, share buybacks inflating the ratio.

## Share Count
- **What it means:** Number of shares outstanding (basic and diluted).
- **Why investors care:** Dilution erodes per-share value.
- **Good vs bad:** Stable or shrinking (buybacks) is good; rising is dilution.
- **Trend:** Watch diluted share count over time.
- **Manipulation:** Aggressive buybacks masking dilution from stock comp.

## Cash
- **What it means:** Cash and equivalents on the balance sheet.
- **Why investors care:** Liquidity, safety, and optionality.
- **Good vs bad:** Net cash is strong; but excess cash can signal poor capital
  allocation.
- **Trend:** Net cash vs net debt position.
- **Manipulation:** Cash trapped overseas, restricted cash.

## EPS (Earnings Per Share)
- **What it means:** Net income / shares outstanding.
- **Why investors care:** Drives valuation multiples and per-share growth.
- **Good vs bad:** Growing EPS with growing quality earnings.
- **Trend:** GAAP vs non-GAAP gap is a red flag.
- **Manipulation:** Buybacks, one-time items, aggressive accounting.
